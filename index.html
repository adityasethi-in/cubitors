A few useful answers

Before we start
working together.

Do you consult or build the solution too?

Both. Cubitors can review a problem and recommend an approach, or take on design, implementation, integration and ongoing application support. The agreed scope defines our responsibility.

Can you work on our existing software and data?

Yes. We can begin with a review of your code, workflows or data processes, then scope troubleshooting, improvements, integrations or a migration. We establish access and requirements before making changes.

Do you work with international businesses?

Our focus is international clients, with delivery from India. We agree communication channels, meeting windows, milestones and the quotation currency before starting.

How are projects priced?

Consulting, scoped projects and ongoing support are quoted around the work required. Your proposal sets out deliverables, fees, currency, payment milestones and any third-party costs. See engagement options ↗

What if we are not sure what solution we need?

Bring the problem rather than a specification. We can start with discovery, review your current setup and define the next step before committing to a larger build.
